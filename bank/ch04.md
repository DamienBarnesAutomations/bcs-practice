# Chapter 4 question bank: The Business Analysis Service Framework

Source: Business Analysis, 4th edition (Paul, Cadle et al., BCS 2020), chapter 4, printed pages 87-103.
Syllabus: BCS Foundation Certificate in Business Analysis, v4.1 (April 2024).

This markdown file is the source of truth. questions.json is generated from bank/*.md by tools/build_bank.py and must not be edited by hand.

Scope: 16 questions, all mapped to LO 4.1. Coverage: the framework as a portfolio of services (3), situation investigation and problem analysis (2), feasibility assessment and business case development (2), business process improvement (2), requirements definition (2), business acceptance testing (1), business change deployment (2), and the two services described as beyond the core (2).

Two notes on scope. The syllabus checklist lists stakeholder engagement under 4.1, but page 97 states that it is not part of the BASF because it is an auxiliary service. No question here turns on whether stakeholder engagement counts as a BASF service; the six services in Figure 4.1 are tested instead. Strategy analysis and execution and IT requirements specification are described as services beyond the core on page 18 of chapter 1; they are tagged 4.1 here because the syllabus maps the service portfolio to that outcome.

Feasibility assessment and business case development and business acceptance testing already have one question each in bank/ch01.md (ch1-4.1-01 and ch1-4.1-02). The questions here take different angles and do not repeat them.

stem: Two weeks before go-live, business managers say they have no time to test and ask the analyst to approve the solution on their behalf. What is the correct position under the business acceptance testing service?
